Arun interface sounds (the same files as Ronak's)
=================================================

Every .mp3 in this folder is taken unchanged from the "soft" pack of uisfx
0.4.0 (https://uisfx.com, npm package "uisfx"), by Yuki Capital.

The uisfx audio files are dedicated to the public domain under the
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode
(SPDX: CC0-1.0). No attribution is required; this note is a courtesy.

Why this pack: it is the one theme in uisfx described as "rounded, warm,
reassuring" and meant for wellness apps, every cue is loudness-matched
(peaks at about -8 dBFS), and the tap sounds are over in 0.15-0.3 s, which
is short enough to feel like the app registering a touch rather than
announcing one.
