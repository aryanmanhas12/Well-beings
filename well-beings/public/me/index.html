The vision — NeuroBioPsych

One mind, three layers. Treat all of them.

Mental health doesn't live in one place. It's neural circuits, it's biology — sleep, stress physiology, what the body is doing — and it's the psychological and social world a person actually lives in. Care that looks at only one layer misses the other two.

Nowhere is that gap more urgent than in India: over a billion people, and a severe shortage of psychiatrists and psychologists to serve them. Most people who need support never receive any. Software won't replace clinicians — but evidence-first tools can screen, guide and support people the system can't yet reach, and can do it privately, in their own language of daily life.

Well-Beings Live

A privacy-first productivity & mental well-being app for teens and young adults. A 5-minute chat-style check-in built on the same short screeners clinicians use (PHQ-2→9, GAD-2→7, sleep, burnout) generates a personalised daily system: a time-blocked schedule, evidence-cited intervention cards, a habit stack, a weekly recovery quota and a burnout radar. Region-local crisis helplines are one tap away, always. Everything runs in the browser — no account, no server, no data leaves the device.
